# Writing style guide

## Voice

Clear, warm, practical, honest. We sound like an experienced teacher who also knows the technology: confident without boasting, specific rather than grand.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Helpful and specific | Salesy or vague |
| Plain-spoken | Full of jargon |
| Honest about what we do | Inflating results |
| Respectful of teachers' time | Lecturing |

## Rules

1. **Reading level:** aim for Class 8–9 (Flesch reading ease 60+). Sentences under 20 words where possible.
2. **Spelling:** Indian/British English — organisation, programme, colour, centre, enrol, licence (noun), practise (verb). Dates: 15 November 2026. Numbers: lakh/crore only if the audience expects it; otherwise use digits with commas (12,000).
3. **No unverified claims.** Every number needs a source and date (in `site.ts`). No "No. 1", "leading", "best", "trusted by thousands" unless independently proven.
4. **Lead with the benefit to students and teachers**, then explain how.
5. **Buttons say what happens:** "Book a consultation", "Request a quote", "Download the guide" — never "Submit" or "Click here".
6. **Links make sense out of context:** "Read the cookie policy", not "here".
7. **Headings are sentence case:** "How we work together", not "How We Work Together".
8. **One idea per paragraph.** Two to four sentences.

## Words to use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| hands-on, practical, build | cutting-edge, revolutionary, next-gen |
| help, support, plan | leverage, synergise, empower (overused) |
| students, learners, teachers | users, end-users |
| AI lab, robotics lab | "smart" lab, "future" lab (unless it is a product name) |
| we reply within one working day | ASAP, 24/7 (unless true) |
| institution, school, college | client (in public copy) |

## Checklist before publishing any page

- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked (LanguageTool, set to English – India / UK)
- [ ] No filler sentences ("In today's fast-paced world…")
- [ ] Every claim is true today and approved
- [ ] One clear next step above and below the fold
- [ ] Meta title ≤ 60 characters and description 120–160 characters, unique
- [ ] Every image has meaningful alt text (or `alt=""` if decorative)

## Visuals

- Use original, locally hosted illustrations or approved photographs. Avoid hotlinked assets.
- Decorative artwork must use empty alt text; photos that show an activity need specific alt text.
- Keep imagery lightweight, responsive and readable against its background.
- Respect reduced-motion settings for animation; never rely on motion to communicate meaning.
