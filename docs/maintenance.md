# Maintenance and content calendar

## Monthly maintenance checklist (first working Monday)

- [ ] Merge Dependabot updates; `npm audit` clean; CI green
- [ ] Check `/internal/placeholders`: open items, last form delivery, funnel drop-off, A/B results
- [ ] Submit a test enquiry and confirm it reaches the CRM
- [ ] Export CRM leads as a backup
- [ ] Search Console: coverage errors, Core Web Vitals, top queries; fix anything new
- [ ] Broken links (CI link check + Search Console 404s)
- [ ] Analytics review: top pages, conversion rate per CTA, form start → submit rate
- [ ] Content review: is every number, team member, testimonial and price still current?
- [ ] Check case-study and photo consents for expiry
- [ ] Every 6 months: rotate secrets (runbook 5), re-run the device/browser matrix, review legal pages

### Suggested monthly rhythm

| Week | Blog / website | Social | Events |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One practical guide (e.g. "Choosing sensors for a school robotics lab") | 3 posts from the guide | — |
| 2 | Case study or project story (with consent) | Photos from a recent session | Announce upcoming event |
| 3 | Teacher-focused tip | Short video of students building | Registration reminders |
| 4 | Month round-up / FAQ | Behind the scenes | Event recap with results |

Academic calendar notes: plan lab-setup campaigns for **Feb–Apr** (budget planning) and **Jun–Jul** (new session); avoid exam months for student events.
